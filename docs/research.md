# Research: What Matters in Relationships

Goal: understand which relationship factors are backed by evidence, which ones an app can realistically influence, and what to build first.

## 1. Strongest evidence

### Joel et al. 2020 (PNAS) — 43 longitudinal datasets, 11,196 couples

Machine learning (random forests) across 43 studies. Top predictors of relationship satisfaction:

1. **Perceived partner commitment**
2. **Appreciation** of partner
3. **Sexual satisfaction**
4. **Perceived partner satisfaction**
5. **Conflict** (level of)

Relationship-specific factors explain roughly 45% of variance in relationship quality; individual traits only ~21%. The *dynamic* matters more than *who* the partner is — and dynamics are changeable.

Insight: #1 and #4 are *perceptions*. An app cannot change how committed someone is, but it can make a partner's care **visible**.

Sources: [Western University](https://news.westernu.ca/2020/07/machine-learning-predicts-satisfaction-in-romantic-relationships/), [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/sliding-vs-deciding/202008/massive-new-study-on-predictors-of-relationship-satisfaction), [IFS](https://ifstudies.org/blog/what-a-groundbreaking-study-on-43-data-sets-reveals-about-relationship-satisfaction)

### Gottman — bids for connection

A "bid" is any attempt to get a partner's attention or connection. In a six-year study of newlyweds, couples who stayed together "turned toward" bids **86%** of the time; couples who divorced, **33%**.

Sources: [FS Blog](https://fs.blog/mind-gym-relationships/), [Couples Therapy Inc](https://couplestherapyinc.com/bids-for-connection-and-turning-toward/)

### Gottman — Sound Relationship House

Foundation layers:

- **Love maps** — knowing your partner's inner world
- **Fondness & admiration**
- **Turning toward** bids
- **5:1 ratio** — stable couples have about five positive interactions per negative one
- **Repair attempts** during conflict

Source: [Couples Therapy Inc](https://couplestherapyinc.com/sound-relationship-house/)

### Gable et al. 2004 — capitalization

How a partner responds to **good news** predicts relationship quality *better* than how they respond to bad news. Best response: active-constructive (genuine enthusiasm + follow-up questions).

Source: [Greater Good in Action](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/capitalizing_on_positive_events)

### Novelty and gratitude

- Couples who do new, challenging activities together report more passion and satisfaction than couples stuck in routine.
- Daily gratitude toward a partner is among the most consistently supported maintenance behaviors.

Sources: [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/a-4-step-maintenance-plan-to-help-keep-your-relationship-going-strong-130736), [Simply Psychology](https://www.simplypsychology.com/articles/long-term-relationship-maintenance-psychology)

## 2. Why couples break up

- Denmark study: lack of love/intimacy (47%), communication problems (44%) — [AllPsych](https://allpsych.com/the-top-reasons-for-divorce/)
- UK divorce lawyers: "growing apart" was #1; cheating 25%; money only ~5% — [goodtoknow](https://www.goodtoknow.co.uk/wellbeing/relationships/common-reasons-for-divorce-105910)
- 886 couples: 53% cited lack of communication — [marriage.com](https://www.marriage.com/advice/relationship/why-do-people-breakup/)

**Pattern:** drift, not explosions. Drift is slow and invisible — exactly what a daily habit can counter.

## 3. Competitor landscape

| App | Users like | Users complain |
|---|---|---|
| Paired (4.6★, ~11k reviews) | Prompts spark real conversations, help reconnect | Notification bugs, unexpected subscription charges |
| Lovewick (4.5★) | Daily questions build intimacy | Aggressive paywall reduced previously free questions |
| Agapé | 99% engagement in study; individual and relationship functioning improved | — |

Takeaways:

- The daily prompt is a proven core — but the category is crowded with it.
- Pricing trust is a weak point for competitors.
- Retention is the real battle (~10% monthly churn is considered healthy in the category).

Sources: [Kimola (Paired reviews)](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-relationship-insights-paired-app-feedback-report-google-play-en-in-154993), [Lovewick overview](https://marlvel.ai/apps/lovewick-relationship-tracker), [Agapé study](https://aub.ie/MilitaryREACH-Rogge2023), [Acquire listing](https://app.acquire.com/public/w01evnw3py-cross-platform-couples-app-with-2-000-questions-and-automated-organic-growth)

## 4. Dating vs. married (hypothesis — to validate in interviews)

| | Dating (0–3 yrs) | Married / long-term |
|---|---|---|
| Core question | "Are we compatible? Where is this going?" | "How do we stay close inside routine?" |
| Main risk | Expectation mismatch, uncertain commitment | Drift, routine, taking each other for granted |
| Wants | Discovery, fun, firsts, milestones, big-topic talks | Novelty, appreciation, intimacy, shared logistics |
| Research hook | Love maps, perceived commitment | Gratitude, novelty, 5:1, bids |

Shared core: **feeling known, feeling appreciated, seeing partner invest.**

## 5. Research → feature mapping

| Factor | App leverage | Feature idea |
|---|---|---|
| Perceived commitment | High | Make effort visible ("Sam answered and reacted to your memory") |
| Appreciation / gratitude | High | Daily one-tap appreciation note, saved to a private "love jar" |
| Love maps | High | Daily question with mutual reveal; "how well do you know me" quizzes |
| Bids / turning toward | Medium | "Thinking of you" tap with easy response |
| Capitalization | Medium | "Good news" post with prompts to celebrate |
| Novelty | Medium | Novel date idea deck, bucket list |
| Sexual satisfaction | Sensitive | Opt-in private desire match (only mutual "yes" revealed) |
| Conflict | Hard | Repair scripts, cool-down button — do not play therapist early |
| Perceived partner satisfaction | Medium | Weekly check-in visible to both |
